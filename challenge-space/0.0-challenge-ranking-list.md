### Why 1.0 is the Next One
- **Priority 1 (MVP value):** Without an article landing page an author cannot see their published paper — the whole purpose of the platform is unserved.
- **Priority 2 (critical-path blocker):** Every downstream challenge (archive pages linking to articles, sitemap entries, crawl verification) depends on the article route existing and on the data-ingestion/contract layer built in 1.0.
- **Priority 3 (zero upstream blockers):** The backend already produces the JSON; nothing blocks us.
- **Priority 4 (MVP necessary):** Yes — article landing is MVP 1 core.
