# Challenge: Journal Directory & Archive Pages

ID: 1.1 · Status: In Progress (built alongside 1.0) · Last Updated: 2026-07-26

## The Core Problem
Provide the crawler and the reader a plain-HTML browse path from `/journals/` → `/journals/[initials]/` → each article. This is the structural backbone of the Part 10 ≤7-click rule and the human analog of it.

## Constraints & Boundaries
- All links are plain `<a href>` (no JS routing, no infinite scroll).
- `/journals/index.astro` lists all founding journals with name, description, ISSN print + online.
- `/journals/[journal_initials]/index.astro` lists ONLY papers whose `journal_initials` matches the route param (strict filter).
- Journal/issue text must be visually smaller than authors (else the Google Scholar fallback parser misidentifies the journal name as the author).

## Inclusion Criteria
- `src/pages/journals/index.astro` (directory).
- `src/pages/journals/[journal_initials]/index.astro` (archive) + `getStaticPaths` over `journals`.
- Strict filter of `papers` by `journal_initials`.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Article page → 1.0.
- Author pages, search → MVP 2+.

## History & Decisive Decisions
- Built in the same pass as 1.0: an article page that links back to a not-yet-existing archive would ship a broken `<a>` — a Part 10 violation. Rounding up both together is cheaper than coordinating two passes.
